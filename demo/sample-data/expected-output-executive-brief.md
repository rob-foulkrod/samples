# Expected Output: COO Decision Brief

Classification: Public

This demo uses a fictional Microsoft Fake Company created for demonstration purposes. Any resemblance to real organizations, people, products, services, or data is coincidental. Do not use customer confidential information in this package.

## Situation

Northstar Grain Exchange has a concentrated 72-hour disruption window across Gulf export, Pacific Northwest rail, interior elevator, and containerized specialty-crop corridors. The highest operational exposure comes from delayed soybean meal and soft red winter wheat shipments with premium customer commitments, limited inventory buffer, and traceability documentation exceptions.

## Recommended decisions

| Decision | Recommendation | Rationale | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reallocate inventory | Move 2,400 MT from Chicago reserve to cover Midwest feed customer commitments | Protects a tier-one customer while reducing demurrage exposure | Operations Manager |
| Expedite documentation | Prioritize chain-of-custody remediation for three high-value lots | Prevents sustainability exception from becoming a delivery hold | Sustainability Analyst |
| Customer communication | Send proactive update to affected bakery and feed accounts today | Preserves trust and creates a clear decision path | Commercial Lead |

## Open questions for operations call

1. Which railcar set can be released without breaching next-week commitments?
2. Can the freight desk secure an alternate vessel slot before demurrage escalates?
3. Which customers require written notice before the end of the business day?
4. Are traceability documents complete enough for conditional release?
5. What exposure threshold requires COO approval?
